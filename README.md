# College Algebra Educational Games

Browser games for practicing College Algebra skills. They run on phones and computers, with nothing to install and no account needed.

**Play here:** https://jlgagnon-glitch.github.io/College-Algebra-Educational-Games/

## Games

| Game | Section | Skills |
|---|---|---|
| [Branch & Box](https://jlgagnon-glitch.github.io/College-Algebra-Educational-Games/games/branch-and-box.html) | 1.5 Factoring | Prime factorization, LCM and GCF, GCF of a polynomial, trinomials with a = 1, factoring by grouping (ac method), difference of squares and perfect square trinomials |
| [Over & Out](https://jlgagnon-glitch.github.io/College-Algebra-Educational-Games/games/over-and-out.html) | 1.6 Rational Expressions | Simplifying with restrictions, multiplying and dividing, adding and subtracting with an LCD, complex fractions, common cancelling mistakes |
| [Plot Twist](https://jlgagnon-glitch.github.io/College-Algebra-Educational-Games/games/plot-twist.html) | 2.1 Rectangular Coordinate System | Plotting points and quadrants, graphing by plotting points, intercepts, distance formula, midpoint formula |
| [Par for the Line](https://jlgagnon-glitch.github.io/College-Algebra-Educational-Games/games/par-for-the-line.html) | 2.2 Linear Equations | Solving linear equations (conditional, identity, inconsistent), rational equations, slope, slope-intercept and point-slope form, parallel and perpendicular lines |
| [i Spy](https://jlgagnon-glitch.github.io/College-Algebra-Educational-Games/games/i-spy.html) | 2.4 Complex Numbers | Simplifying radicals and square roots of negatives, adding and subtracting, multiplying, conjugates and dividing, powers of i |
| [Zero Hero](https://jlgagnon-glitch.github.io/College-Algebra-Educational-Games/games/zero-hero.html) | 2.5 Quadratic Equations | Zero product property, square root method, quadratic formula and discriminant, completing the square |

### Branch & Box

There are six stations, one for each skill in Section 1.5. Each station has five randomly generated rounds. A round with no mistakes earns three stars, and each mistake costs one. Station 6 is a 60-second timed round. Stars are saved in the player's own browser only.

### Over & Out

Factors appear as tiles above and below the fraction bar. Players cancel a factor on top with the same factor on the bottom. Opposite factors such as (7 − x) and (x − 7) do not cancel until −1 is factored out. There are five stations with five randomly generated rounds each, scored the same way as Branch & Box. Station 5 is a 60-second timed round.

### Plot Twist

Players answer by tapping a coordinate grid that shows the coordinates of the point under the pointer. Stations cover plotting and quadrants, graphing from a table, intercepts, distance (by building the right triangle), and midpoints, plus a 60-second quadrant round.

### Par for the Line

Golf-themed. Linear equations are solved on a balance in as few moves as possible (par), and then labeled conditional, identity, or inconsistent. Other stations clear fractions in rational equations, find slopes from rise and run, build lines that pass through flags on the grid, and finish with a 60-second parallel-or-perpendicular round.

### i Spy

Complex numbers are shown on the complex plane, with the real part across and the imaginary part up. Players pull pairs of factors (and −1 as i) out of square roots, place sums and differences on the plane, change i² to −1 in a FOIL box, divide using conjugates, and finish with a 60-second powers-of-i round on a quarter-turn dial.

### Zero Hero

Players solve quadratics by factoring, the square root method, the quadratic formula, and completing the square (with algebra tiles that fill in the missing corner). Each solved equation ends with its parabola crossing the x-axis at the solutions, or missing it when the solutions are complex. The last station is a 60-second discriminant round.

## Repository layout

- `games/` holds the games. Each one is a single, self-contained HTML file.
