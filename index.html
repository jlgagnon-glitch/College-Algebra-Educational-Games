College Algebra Educational Games

Browser games for practicing College Algebra skills. They run on phones and computers, with nothing to install and no account needed.

  • Section 1.5 · Factoring

    Branch & Box

    Six stations: prime factor trees, LCM and GCF, pulling out the GCF, trinomials with a = 1, factoring by grouping, and a 60-second special-cases round.

    Play →
  • Section 1.6 · Rational Expressions

    Over & Out

    Five stations: factor and cancel with restrictions, multiplying and dividing, adding and subtracting with an LCD, complex fractions, and a 60-second legal-or-illegal cancelling round.

    Play →
  • Section 2.1 · Rectangular Coordinate System

    Plot Twist

    Six stations on a tap-to-plot grid: plotting points and quadrants, graphing from a table, intercepts, the distance formula, midpoints, and a 60-second quadrant round.

    Play →
  • Section 2.2 · Linear Equations

    Par for the Line

    Five golf-themed stations: solve equations in par, clear fractions in rational equations, find slopes, build lines through flags, and a 60-second parallel-or-perpendicular round.

    Play →
  • Section 2.4 · Complex Numbers

    i Spy

    Five stations: pull pairs and i out of square roots, add and subtract on the complex plane, FOIL with i² = −1, divide using conjugates, and a 60-second powers-of-i round.

    Play →
  • Section 2.5 · Quadratic Equations

    Zero Hero

    Five stations: solve by factoring, the square root method, the quadratic formula, and completing the square with tiles, then a 60-second discriminant round. Each solution shows up on the parabola.

    Play →
Progress (stars) is saved in your own browser only.
